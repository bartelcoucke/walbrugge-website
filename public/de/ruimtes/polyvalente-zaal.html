Mehrzwecksaal

Our largest space with an interactive whiteboard for hybrid meetings

Room for big ideas

The multi-purpose room is our largest and most flexible meeting space. With a capacity of up to 40 Personen in theatre layout, it is the ideal venue for seminars, team days and corporate presentations.

The room is equipped with a professional interactive whiteboard with integrated webcam — perfect for hybrid meetings where part of your team joins remotely. The flexible layout adapts to your programme: from theatre hall to workshop space.

Ideal für

  • Seminars & conferences (up to 40 Personen)
  • Team days & staff meetings
  • Hybride Tagungen with remote participants
  • Product presentations & launches
  • Training for larger groups
  • Client days & corporate events

Ausstattung

  • 📺 Professional interactive whiteboard with webcam
  • 🎤 Wireless microphone available
  • 📶 Free high-speed WiFi
  • 🪑 Flexible layout (theatre/U-shape/islands)
  • ☕ Coffee, tea, water included
  • 🅿️ Spacious free parking

💡 Tip: combine with catering

Combine the room with our lunch packages (from €14 p.p.) for a complete team day. Catering from our own kitchen with fresh, seasonal produce.
