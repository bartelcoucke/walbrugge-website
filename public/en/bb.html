Finding peace on a working estate

The five guest rooms at domein Walbrugge are each named after a plant from our food forest. Every room tells its own story — from the authentic beams to the carefully chosen details. No standard hotel room, but a place with character.

Outside, the Tiegemberg awaits: walking trails start literally from the terrace. In the morning, breakfast is served with fresh produce, homemade jam and — in season — fruit from our own garden.

Breakfast

Wake up to the best of the season

Enjoying breakfast at Domein Walbrugge is something you do at your own pace. Between 8 and 10 am, a richly laid table awaits with fresh bread rolls, homemade jam, local cheeses, charcuterie and — depending on the season — fruit and vegetables from our own garden.

  • Breakfast included in the price
  • Fresh produce from our own garden
  • Local cheeses and charcuterie
  • Homemade jam
  • Vegetarian options
