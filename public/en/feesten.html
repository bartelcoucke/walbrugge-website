Where memories are made

Domein Walbrugge is no ordinary party venue. It is a working estate at the foot of the Tiegemberg, where the charm of an 18th-century square farmstead meets modern comfort. When you celebrate here, the entire estate is exclusively yours — no other guests, no shared spaces.

Outside catering welcome: bring your own caterer or use our kitchen. We also offer homemade options with produce from our own garden.

💒

Wedding

The most beautiful day of your life, in the most beautiful place

Domein Walbrugge offers everything for an unforgettable wedding. Begin with an intimate ceremony in the food forest among the fruit trees and herbs. Enjoy the reception on the sunny terrace, dine in the atmospheric multi-purpose hall, and dance into the small hours in the romantically lit courtyard.

  • Seated dinner for up to 100 guests
  • Ceremony in the food forest or courtyard
  • Evening party under the stars
  • Overnight stay for the couple + guests (5 rooms)
  • Your own caterer or our partners

Celebrate every milestone in style

Whether it is a communion party, a spring celebration, a milestone birthday or a well-deserved retirement party — domein Walbrugge offers the perfect setting for family celebrations.
