Parties & Celebrations

Your celebration in a place
you will never forget

Unique wedding parties, communions, birthdays and joyful baby showers in an authentic 18th-century farmstead. Exclusively for you and your guests.

🏆 Salino Award · Best event venue 2025 | 10/10 on Eventplanner

Where unforgettable memories are made

Spacious enough to dine in comfort, cosy enough to keep it intimate. The banquet hall is set in a beautiful 18th-century square farmstead with modern comfort.
On your day, the banquet hall, the inner patio and the sprawling garden are exclusively yours.
A reception in the summer evening sun, children running through the sprawling garden, ...

Catering is entirely up to you: your own caterer, a food truck in the car park or our homemade options with produce from our own garden.

And those who want to keep the evening going can stay the night in style. We have five luxury B&B rooms, each for two people.

Wedding

The most beautiful day of your lives, in the most beautiful place

Domein Walbrugge offers everything for an unforgettable wedding. Begin with an intimate ceremony in the food forest among the flowers and fruit trees. Enjoy the reception on the sunny terrace, dine in the atmospheric, beautiful wedding hall, and dance into the small hours on the romantically lit inner patio.

  • Seated dinner for up to 60 guests, up to 100 guests with a walking dinner
  • Ceremony in the food forest or on the inner patio
  • Evening party under the stars
  • Overnight stay for the couple + guests possible in our luxury B&B (5 rooms)
  • Your own caterer or our partners
