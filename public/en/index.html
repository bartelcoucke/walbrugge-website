An 18th-century square farmstead with a rich history and an inspiring future.

Domein Walbrugge is a working estate at the foot of the Tiegemberg, on the border of the Flemish Ardennes and south-west Flanders. In recent years the farmstead has been transformed into an event venue, B&B and meeting location — without losing the character that makes it special.

Whether you come for a celebration, a stay or a meeting, the experience is the same: a place unlike any other in the region. That is no coincidence. It is the combination of a historic setting, a food forest, artisanal catering and one of Flanders' most beautiful ridges nearby that makes domein Walbrugge what it is.
