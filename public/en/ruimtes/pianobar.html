Piano Bar

The stylish spot for receptions and networking drinks

Where business becomes informal

The piano bar at domein Walbrugge is the ideal space for the informal part of your business day. After an intensive meeting or training session, the bar offers a relaxed atmosphere for an aperitif, networking drinks or a walking dinner.

The stylish interior — with the characteristic piano at its centre — creates exactly the right ambience: professional enough for business conversations, warm enough for genuine connections.

Ideal for

  • Aperitif after a meeting
  • Informal team gatherings
  • Walking dinner
  • Closing a client event
