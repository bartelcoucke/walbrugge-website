Meeting & Training Room

The ideal space for trainers, coaches and workshop leaders

Where learning and collaboration take centre stage

Our meeting and training room is specifically designed for training courses, workshops and intensive work sessions. The space offers excellent acoustics and pleasant natural light — exactly what you need for a productive day.

The flexible layout adapts to your programme: from classroom setting to interactive workshop groups. The intimate scale (max. 20 people) ensures optimal interaction between trainer and participants.
