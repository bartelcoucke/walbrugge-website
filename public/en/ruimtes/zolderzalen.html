Attic Rooms

Characterful spaces beneath the authentic roof beams

Where creativity gets room to breathe

Our attic rooms are located beneath the characterful roof beams of the 18th-century farmstead. The authentic atmosphere — exposed timber structures, warm materials and unique acoustics — makes this an inspiring environment for creative work.

The attic rooms can be split into smaller breakout spaces or used as one large room for bigger groups. Ideal for team building, brainstorms or workshops where the setting is part of the experience.

Ideal for

  • Creative workshops & brainstorms
  • Team building sessions
  • Seminars with breakout spaces
  • Design thinking sessions
  • Strategy days in a unique setting
  • Yoga & mindfulness retreats

Facilities

  • 🏛️ Authentic roof beams & character
  • 📺 Screen/projector available
  • 📋 Flipchart & materials
  • 📶 Free high-speed WiFi
  • ☕ Coffee, tea, water included
  • 🔀 Divisible into breakout spaces
