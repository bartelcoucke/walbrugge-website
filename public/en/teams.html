Sustainability

Meetings with respect for tomorrow

Domein Walbrugge is more than a venue. It is a working estate where sustainability is not a marketing term, but daily practice.

🌱

Food forest

Catering with ingredients from our own garden and food forest. Seasonal, local, without long supply chains.

☀️

Solar panels

The estate runs on solar power. Your meeting has a minimal CO₂ footprint.

🔥

Heat pump

Heating and passive cooling via geothermal energy. No fossil fuels for climate control.

⚡

6 charging points

Charge electric cars at 22 kW charging points. Your team drives home CO₂-neutral.

💧

Rainwater harvesting

Sanitary facilities and irrigation run on collected rainwater. Drinking water only where needed.

🚫

No pesticides

The estate is managed ecologically. No pesticides, no chemical fertilisers.

Your ESG reporting? We are happy to provide a sustainability certificate for your event.
